# Forgeload early test: guide for testers

Thanks for trying Forgeload. It's an early test, so things will break and that's useful. Please be blunt.

**Link:** https://forgeload.vercel.app (add it to your home screen: iPhone Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen; Android Chrome → menu → Install app).

## What to do (about two gym sessions)
1. Create an account and confirm your email.
2. Answer the five questions (or build your own plan if you already have one).
3. Look over the plan it builds. Replace at least one exercise you don't like (Plan → tap an exercise → Replace).
4. Log a real workout at the gym. Use the rest timer. Try tapping a set to undo it and log it again.
5. On another day, log a second workout. Check that it shows what you did last time and a target.
6. Fill in the short feedback form (your organizer will send it).

## Good to know
- **Saving:** each set is stored on your phone the moment you tap ✓, then sent to your account. A dashed ◌ means "on this phone, not synced yet"; a solid ✓ means "saved to your account". If you lose signal it syncs when you're back. Please don't clear your browser data or use a private window mid-workout, because that would remove sets that haven't synced yet.
- **Starting a workout needs a connection.** Logging sets during it doesn't.
- **Your data:** stored in your own account. Nobody else can see it. Settings → Export downloads everything; Settings → Delete account removes it all.
- **Problems:** screenshot it and send it to your organizer with what you tapped just before.
