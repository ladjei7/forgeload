# Tester feedback form (about 5 minutes)

Use a Google Form or similar. Ratings are 1 (poor) to 5 (great); always keep the "why" box.

1. What do you use now to track workouts? (Strong / Hevy / another app / notes or spreadsheet / nothing)
2. Getting started was easy. (1-5) What slowed you down?
3. The plan it built fit you. (1-5) Anything you'd change?
4. Finding and replacing an exercise was easy. (1-5)
5. Logging a set between sets was fast. (1-5) Anything that got in the way?
6. In your own words, what did "Target" mean on the workout screen?
7. Did the "why this target?" explanation make sense? (yes / no / didn't open it)
8. Did your workouts move on the way you expected after a missed day? (yes / no / didn't miss one) Explain.
9. Did you trust that your sets were saved? (1-5) Did anything make you doubt it?
10. How does it look? (1-5)
11. What was the most confusing moment?
12. What's missing that you'd need to use it every week?
13. Would you keep using Forgeload instead of what you use now? (yes / maybe / no) Why?
14. What would make you switch for good, and what would stop you?
15. Anything else?
